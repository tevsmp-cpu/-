# Bathroom Design Specifications

## Project Context
- **Room Width:** 2200 mm
- **Fixtures:** 2 Shower cabins (1000x1000 mm each), 1 Back-to-wall bath (1700x780 mm).
- **Status:** Technical design and plumbing points are fixed.

---

## Variant 1: Eco-Minimalism (Light Stone & Wood)
**Concept:** Visual expansion of space through light textures and natural materials.

### Materials & Textures
- **Walls/Floor:** Large-format porcelain tiles (60x120 or 120x120 cm) with light travertine or warm concrete texture.
- **Accents:** Natural oak-effect slatted panels in shower niches and behind the bath (moisture-resistant).
- **Partitions:** Transparent "Optiwhite" glass for shower cabins to maintain visual openness.

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Warm Beige (#D6CFC7), Light Grey (#E5E4E2).
- **Accent:** Natural Oak (#BC9E82).
- **Fixtures:** Polished Chrome or Brushed Nickel.

### Lighting
- **Ambient:** Recessed ceiling LED spots (3000K).
- **Accent:** Hidden waterproof LED strips in shower niches and under the bath rim for a "floating" effect.

---

## Variant 2: Modern Industrial (Graphite & Glass)
**Concept:** Strict geometry and deep textures for a sophisticated, masculine atmosphere.

### Materials & Textures
- **Walls/Floor:** Dark anthracite slate-effect porcelain tiles with pronounced texture.
- **Accents:** Black metal profiles for shower partitions, microcement textures on non-tiled areas.
- **Partitions:** Clear glass with thin black matte frames.

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Anthracite (#383E42), Deep Grey (#4A4E52).
- **Accent:** Matte Black (#28282B).
- **Fixtures:** Matte Black.

### Lighting
- **Ambient:** Directional magnetic track lighting in black.
- **Accent:** Vertical LED lines in corners and spotlighting on the slate texture to emphasize relief.
